---
title: Approvals
category: team
summary: Review changes before they go live.
context: [reviews]
order: 20
---

On websites with approvals, changes of editors do not go online at once. They wait under
**Approvals** until an owner looks at them.

- Every waiting change has a **preview** in the real design of your website.
- *Approve* publishes it, *Decline* discards it.
- Opening a page with a waiting change shows a note.
