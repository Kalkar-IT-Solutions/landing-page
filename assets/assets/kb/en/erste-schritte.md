Welcome to Redaktion. As an owner you see a list of **first steps** on the home of your
website. What Redaktion detects itself is ticked already; tick the rest once it is done.

1. **Set a password**: you chose one through the invitation link. Change it any time under
   *Account*, or add a passkey.
2. **Invite a second person**: so your website does not depend on one person, invite a
   colleague under *Access* ([more](kb:team)).
3. **Publish a first change**: open a section, change a text and tap *Publish*
   ([more](kb:veroeffentlichen)).
4. **Check the imprint**: are address, contact and representation right? ([more](kb:impressum))
5. **Read the help**: you found it.

You can hide the list once you no longer need it.
