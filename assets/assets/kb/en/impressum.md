Every business website in Germany needs a complete **imprint** (§ 5 DDG): name and address,
representation, e-mail and phone, register and VAT number where they apply.

- Open the *Imprint* section and check every entry.
- When something changes (a move, new management), update it right away.
- The **privacy policy** is one of your own pages. If a new service is added (contact form,
  statistics), let us know.

Redaktion is no legal advice.
