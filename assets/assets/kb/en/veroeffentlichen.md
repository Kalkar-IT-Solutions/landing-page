summary: When changes are live and how to bring back an earlier state.
context: [publish]

---

*Publish* saves your change as one step in the history of your website. The server then
builds the site; after about a minute it is online. The symbol at the top right shows whether
the build runs, finished or failed.

- **History**: every change is recorded with person and time. An earlier state can be
  restored.
- **Approvals**: if your website is set up that way, a change waits for an owner's approval
  ([more](kb:freigaben)).
