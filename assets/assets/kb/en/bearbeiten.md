title: Editing content
category: content
summary: Fields, drafts and what happens when you save.
context: [editor]
order: 10
---

Each section of your website (news, events, pages, team …) has its own form. Fields are
named as they appear on the website.

- **Required fields** are marked; without them you cannot publish.
- **Texts** are written with the toolbar: bold, italic, lists, links. The preview shows how
  the text looks.
- **Draft**: with the box ticked the entry is saved but not shown.
- **A date in the future**: the post appears on that day.

Until you publish, everything stays on your device. Leaving a page with unsaved changes
asks first.
