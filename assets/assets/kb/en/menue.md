title: Ordering the menu
category: content
summary: Put your own pages into the menu and change their order.
context: [menu]

---

In the **Menu** screen you drag your own pages into the order you want and switch them into
the menu or out of it. Everything is saved in one step.

The fixed sections (news, team …) keep their places; Kalkar IT arranges those.
