title: Adding images
category: content
summary: Upload images, pick them from the library and describe them well.
context: [images]

---

Upload images right in the form or pick one from the **media library**, which shows every
image of your website.

- Redaktion scales large photos down and removes location and camera data before they reach
  the website.
- Give every image a **description**: what does it show? People who cannot see it and search
  engines read it.
- JPEG, PNG, GIF and WebP up to 8 MB are allowed.
