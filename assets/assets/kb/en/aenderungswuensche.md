Some things go beyond content: a new kind of page, another layout, a form. That is what
**change requests** are for.

1. Describe your wish: a title, what should change and, if it is about a page, its address.
   Screenshots help (up to 5 MB per image, five images).
2. Kalkar IT answers with an **estimate**: hours or a fixed price, often with a date.
3. You **accept** or **decline**. Work starts only after you accept.
4. You see when the work is running and when it is done.

Both sides ask questions in the thread below. Every answer also shows under
*Notifications*.
